Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I still purchase UserPacn 2018 standalone licenses?
A: Yes. Legacy standalone licenses for UserPacn 2018 and prior versions remain available for purchase on our Store page.
Q: Can I cancel my UserPacn 2019 subscription at any time?
A: Absolutely. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime directly through your Xenosoft Account dashboard with no cancellation fees.
Q: What happens to my project files if my subscription expires?
A: Your files remain entirely yours and are stored locally. While an active subscription is required to edit them within Xenosoft apps, your raw exports and data remain intact.
Q: Is offline access supported in UserPacn 2019?
A: Yes. You can work fully offline for up to 30 consecutive days. The system only requires a brief internet connection once a month to verify your active subscription status.
